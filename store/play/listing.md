# Google Play store listing: Sniffd

## App name (30 max)
Sniffd: Ad Scam Checker

## Short description (80 max)
Share a shop ad to Sniffd and see if the store checks out before you buy.

## Full description (4000 max)
Spotted a deal on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook that looks too good to be true? Sniff it first.

Share any shop ad or store link to Sniffd and get a straight answer in seconds: CLEARED, SUSPECT or BUSTED, with every reason spelled out.

HOW IT WORKS
1. On the ad, tap Shop now.
2. Tap Share, then Sniffd.
3. Sniffd checks the store and shows you what it found.

WHAT SNIFFD CHECKS
• How old the store's web address really is (scam stores are usually weeks old)
• Whether an old address was bought and reused for a brand-new shop
• Who's behind it: a real phone number, street address and email
• The refund policy, and what the returns page actually promises
• Signs it ships from overseas: slow shipping, dropshipping tools, Chinese registrars and email
• Pressure tactics: countdown timers, "only 3 left", "closing down" sales
• Too-good-to-be-true discounts on brand-new stores
• Look-alike addresses that copy real brands
• Google's list of dangerous websites
• The Internet Archive's history of the site
• What other buyers reported after ordering

EVERY ANSWER COMES WITH RECEIPTS
Sniffd never just says "trust me." Tap any reason to see why it matters, then decide for yourself.

FREE, NO ACCOUNT
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Sniffd only uses the link you share with it.

HELP THE NEXT PERSON
Ordered from a store? Tell Sniffd whether it arrived. Real buyer reports count more than anything else.

Sniffd works the public record and can't read a seller's mind. Even when a store checks out, pay with a method that offers buyer protection.
