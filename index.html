Add Brisket to your home screen and it shows up in your phone's Share menu.

Check an ad in three taps

  1. On the Instagram ad, tap Shop now. The store opens inside Instagram.
  2. Tap the ⋯ menu at the top of that page, then Share.
  3. Pick Brisket. The receipts come back in a few seconds.

Brisket only shows up in your Share menu after you install it: Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app (not "Add shortcut"). iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen, then paste links here.

Shortcut: in Instagram, Settings → Open links in external browser. Then Shop now opens in Chrome and you share from there.
