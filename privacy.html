Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version: Sniffd has no accounts and doesn't ask who you are. It saves the links you check so results can be shared and improved. It doesn't sell data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

Who we are

Sniffd (the website at getsniffd.com and the Sniffd apps) is run by PJP Labs. Questions about this policy: support@getsniffd.com.

What we collect

  • The link you check. When you check an ad or store, we save the link, its website address, the verdict, the score and the reasons we found. Each check gets a short code so you can share the result.
  • Your feedback, if you give it. If you tell us how a purchase turned out (for example "arrived fine" or "never arrived"), we save that answer with the website it was about. Other people checking the same store see a count of these answers.
  • Basic technical logs. Our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address and device type, for a short time to keep the service running and stop abuse.

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details. If you share a whole message or post with Sniffd, the app only uses the web link inside it.

What stays on your device

Sniffd remembers a few settings on your device, like whether you've closed the how-to tips. They never leave your phone.

Who else sees the link

To check a store, Sniffd looks it up in public sources. These services receive the website address being checked, never information about you:

  • Google Safe Browsing (known dangerous websites)
  • The Internet Archive (past copies of the website)
  • Public domain registration records (RDAP) and DNS lookups through Google Public DNS
  • The Tranco list (website popularity) and ipwho.is (where a website is hosted)
  • The store's own website

The website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the page loads.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data
  • Show ads or use advertising trackers
  • Track you across other apps or websites

How long we keep it

Checks and feedback are kept so shared results keep working and so Sniffd learns which stores cause problems. They aren't linked to you. Server logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider, usually within a few days.

Shared results are public

Anyone with a result's link or code can see that result: the link checked, the verdict and the reasons. Don't check links that contain private information, such as an order page with your name in the address.

Children

Sniffd isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because checks aren't tied to an account, we usually can't tell which ones are yours. If you want a specific result removed, email support@getsniffd.com with its link or code and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
