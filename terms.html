What Sniffd is

Sniffd gives an automated opinion about an online store or ad, based on public information: domain history, the store's own pages, Google Safe Browsing, and what other buyers reported. A verdict is a guide to help you decide, not a guarantee and not a statement of fact about any business.

What Sniffd can't promise
