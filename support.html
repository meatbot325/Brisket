Support

Questions, a result that looks wrong, or a store we should know about? Email support@getsniffd.com. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I check an ad?

In Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, tap Share on the ad or post, then choose Sniffd. You can also copy the link and paste it on the Sniffd home page.

What do the results mean?

  • CLEARED (green): nothing worrying turned up in the public record.
  • SUSPECT (yellow): some warning signs. Read the reasons before you buy.
  • BUSTED (red): serious red flags, like a brand-new store copying a known brand or a website on Google's danger list.

Every result lists the reasons, so you can judge for yourself.

I think a result is wrong

Email us the result's link and tell us what you know about the store. Corrections improve the checks for everyone.

I already bought from a store that looks like a scam

  • Contact your bank or card issuer and ask about a chargeback or dispute.
  • If you paid through PayPal, Shop Pay or a similar service, open a claim there.
  • Report the ad to the app where you saw it.
  • In the US, you can report fraud at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

Removing a result

Email us the result's link or code and we'll delete it. See the privacy policy for what Sniffd saves.
